
1. Create a Wall Holder

• Right-click in your Hierarchy window.
• Select Create Empty and name it Boundaries.
• Reset its Position to (0, 0, 0) in the Inspector.

2. Add individual Wall Colliders

Inside that empty Boundaries GameObject, you will create four child objects (one for each side of the screen):
• Right-click on Boundaries -> Create Empty and name it LeftWall.
• Click Add Component in the Inspector and choose Box Collider 2D.
• Repeat this step to create RightWall, TopWall, and BottomWall.

3. Position and Scale the Colliders

Select each wall object and use the Box Collider 2D's Size and Offset settings (or the Unity Rect/Move tools) to stretch them along the edges of your background sprite:
• Top/Bottom Walls: Make the Size.x very wide (matching or exceeding your background width) and Size.y thin (e.g., 0.5 or 1). Position them exactly at the upper and lower limits of your background.
• Left/Right Walls: Make the Size.y very tall (matching or exceeding your background height) and Size.x thin. Position them exactly at the left and right limits.

4. Configure Physics Settings

For the collision to work perfectly with your snake, ensure the following settings are met:
• On the Walls: Ensure Is Trigger is unchecked on all four Box Collider 2Ds (this makes them physical barriers).
• On the Snake Head:
	• It must have a Rigidbody 2D component.
	• Set the Rigidbody 2D's Gravity Scale to 0.
	• Set Collision Detection to Continuous (prevents the snake from glitching through the wall at high speeds).
	• Expand Constraints and check Freeze Rotation Z to stop the snake from spinning when hitting walls.
	• It must have its own Collider 2D (like a Box Collider 2D or Circle Collider 2D).
